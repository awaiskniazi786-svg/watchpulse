About WatchPulse

Edited by Alex Mercer & the WatchPulse Editorial Team • Updated November 21, 2026

What is WatchPulse?

WatchPulse (watchpulse.online) is an independent movie and TV discovery guide and editorial publication created to help viewers pick something genuinely worth watching without scrolling endlessly.

WatchPulse combines a 30-second mood-based Movie & TV Finder, a TV series catalog (via TVmaze), open film details (via Wikipedia & Wikidata), official studio trailers (via YouTube), a curated film showcase, and 27 original Editorial Guides & Reviews. You can look up cast, directors, runtimes, genres, and official reference links, watch official trailers, and save titles to a private local browser watchlist.

Independent Guide & Data Attribution

WatchPulse is an independent discovery guide and editorial publication. We are not a streaming provider, do not host or stream video files, and are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+, Apple TV+, HBO Max, or any other streaming platform mentioned in our guides. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.

We do not provide per-country streaming availability feeds—because licensing windows change frequently by region, always confirm current playback directly inside your local streaming app. Our open data stack consists of:

  • TVmaze API (CC BY-SA): TV series listings, primary broadcast/streaming network tags, cast lists, runtimes, and show artwork.
  • Wikipedia (CC BY-SA) & Wikidata (CC0 Public Domain): Film summaries, directors, cast, genres, runtimes, release dates, IMDb reference links, and artwork.
  • YouTube: Official studio and network trailers played via YouTube's privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com) or linked directly on YouTube.
  • WatchPulse Original Editorial: Hand-curated film notes, runtime comparisons, and 27 long-form guides & reviews written specifically for WatchPulse readers.

Editorial Desk & Engineering

WatchPulse is led by Lead Editor Alex Mercer alongside the independent WatchPulse Editorial & Engineering Team. Built on high-speed edge architecture with Cloudflare Pages & Edge Functions, WatchPulse integrates the TVmaze API (CC BY-SA), Wikipedia (CC BY-SA), Wikidata (CC0 Public Domain), official YouTube studio trailers, and an original hand-curated editorial library.

Every guide and review is researched, fact-checked against official theatrical runtimes and episode counts, and written to help viewers spend less time scrolling and more time watching great cinema.
