Edited by Alex Mercer & the WatchPulse Editorial Team • Updated November 21, 2026

What is WatchPulse?

WatchPulse (watchpulse.online) is an independent movie and TV discovery guide and editorial publication created to help viewers pick something worth watching without scrolling endlessly.

WatchPulse combines a 30-second mood-based Movie & TV Finder, a live TV series catalog (via TVmaze), open film details (via Wikipedia & Wikidata), official studio trailers (via YouTube), a 197-film curated catalog, and 28 original articles (25 editorial guides and 3 home-viewing reviews) in our Editorial Guides hub. You can look up cast, directors, runtimes, genres, and official reference links, watch official trailers, and save titles to a private local browser watchlist.

Independent Guide & Data Attribution

WatchPulse is an independent discovery guide and editorial publication. We are not a streaming provider, do not host or stream video files, and are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+, Apple TV+, HBO Max, or any other streaming platform mentioned in our guides. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.

We do not provide per-country streaming availability feeds—because licensing windows change frequently by region, always confirm current playback directly inside your local streaming app. Our open data stack consists of:

  • TVmaze API (CC BY-SA): TV series listings, primary broadcast/streaming network tags, cast lists, runtimes, and show artwork.
  • Wikipedia (CC BY-SA) & Wikidata (CC0 Public Domain): Film summaries, directors, cast, genres, runtimes, release dates, IMDb reference links, and artwork.
  • YouTube: Official studio and network trailers played via YouTube's privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com) or linked directly on YouTube.
  • WatchPulse Original Editorial: 197 hand-curated film notes, runtime comparisons, and 28 original articles (25 guides & 3 reviews) written specifically for WatchPulse readers.

Editorial Standards, Independence & Corrections Policy

WatchPulse is an independent publication founded and edited by Alex Mercer. Rather than acting as an automated newsroom or studio affiliate portal, WatchPulse publishes a focused catalog of 197 curated films alongside 25 guides and 3 in-depth reviews hosted on Cloudflare Pages.

  • No Paid Placements or Affiliate Steering: Titles are selected strictly on pacing, craft, audience fit, and rewatch value. We do not accept payment from studios or streaming services to include or rank titles.
  • Fact-Checking & Runtime Verification: Theatrical runtimes, release years, episode counts, directors, and primary network tags are cross-checked against Wikipedia, Wikidata, TVmaze, and official studio trailers before publication.
  • Honest "Skip If" Framing: Great recommendations also say who a movie or show is not for. Our editorial updates explicitly note slow burns, tonal extremes, and licensing caveats rather than hyping every title.
  • Corrections Policy: If you spot an outdated runtime, episode count, broken trailer embed, or regional streaming error, report it via our Contact Page or email contact@watchpulse.online. Verified corrections are applied within 24–48 hours and reflected in the article's visible update date.
