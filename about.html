About WatchPulse

Built & edited by Awais K • Updated November 21, 2026

What is WatchPulse?

WatchPulse (watchpulse.online) is an independent movie and TV discovery guide and editorial publication created to help viewers pick something genuinely worth watching without scrolling endlessly.

WatchPulse combines a TV series catalog (via TVmaze), open film details (via Wikipedia & Wikidata), official studio trailers (via YouTube), a curated film showcase, and 20 original Editorial Guides & Reviews. You can look up cast, directors, runtimes, genres, and official reference links, watch official trailers, and save titles to a private local browser watchlist.

Independent Guide & Data Attribution

WatchPulse is an independent discovery guide and editorial publication. We are not a streaming provider, do not host or stream video files, and are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+, Apple TV+, HBO Max, or any other streaming platform mentioned in our guides. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.

We do not provide per-country streaming availability feeds—because licensing windows change frequently by region, always confirm current playback directly inside your local streaming app. Our open data stack consists of:

  • TVmaze API (CC BY-SA): TV series listings, primary broadcast/streaming network tags, cast lists, runtimes, and show artwork.
  • Wikipedia (CC BY-SA) & Wikidata (CC0 Public Domain): Film summaries, directors, cast, genres, runtimes, release dates, IMDb reference links, and artwork.
  • YouTube: Official studio and network trailers played via YouTube's privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com) or linked directly on YouTube.
  • WatchPulse Original Editorial: Hand-curated film notes, runtime comparisons, and 20 long-form guides & reviews written specifically for WatchPulse readers.

Key Features

🎙️ Unified Search & Voice Search

Search TV series, curated films, and open Wikipedia/Wikidata film profiles from one bar, with typo tolerance and browser voice search.

🎬 Official YouTube Trailers

In-page privacy-enhanced YouTube trailer player verified against official studio, network, and licensed trailer channels, with direct YouTube links.

📚 20 Original Editorial Guides

Long-form curated lists, platform comparisons, runtime picks, and home-viewing reviews written specifically for WatchPulse readers.

📱 Mobile-First Private Watchlist

Responsive layouts with bottom mobile navigation and a local browser watchlist (localStorage) that requires zero account sign-up.
