About WatchPulse

Built & edited by Awais K • Updated November 21, 2026

What is WatchPulse?

WatchPulse (watchpulse.online) was created to solve a common streaming frustration: scrolling through endless algorithm rows without knowing which movies and series are actually worth your evening—or which subscription catalogs fit your taste.

WatchPulse pairs a live TV series directory (powered by TVmaze) and a curated film catalog with 20+ original Editorial Streaming Guides & Reviews. You can browse live TV series with cast and primary network details, explore hand-curated film picks, watch official YouTube trailers, filter by genre and language, and save titles to a private browser watchlist.

Independent Guide & Data Attribution

WatchPulse is an independent discovery guide and editorial publication. We are not a streaming provider, do not host or stream video files, and are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+, Apple TV+, HBO Max, or any other streaming platform listed on our site. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.

Live TV series metadata, schedules, cast lists, and artwork are provided by TVmaze API under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike (CC BY-SA) license. Movie collections, runtime guides, and reviews are original WatchPulse editorial content supplemented with open film facts from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA).

Key Features

🎙️ Unified Search & Voice Search

Search live TV shows, WatchPulse curated films, and open Wikipedia film references from one bar, with browser voice-search support.

🎬 Official YouTube Trailers

In-page privacy-enhanced YouTube trailer modal for featured titles, plus direct YouTube links for any movie or series.

📚 20+ Original Editorial Guides

Long-form curated lists, platform comparisons, runtime picks, and home-viewing reviews written specifically for WatchPulse readers.

📱 Mobile-First Private Watchlist

Responsive layouts with bottom mobile navigation and a local browser watchlist (localStorage) that requires zero account sign-up.
