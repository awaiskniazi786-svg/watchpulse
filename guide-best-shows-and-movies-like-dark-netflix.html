8 Mind-Bending Shows & Movies Like Dark (Ranked by Timeline Craft & Payoff)

By Alex Mercer • Editorial Guide • Updated November 23, 2026

Quick Answer: What Should You Watch First After Finishing Dark?

Finishing Netflix's German labyrinth Dark leaves a very specific hangover: most mystery shows feel simplistic afterward. Rather than dumping generic time-travel titles together, we picked eight series and films that match Dark's interlocking timelines, moody dread, and disciplined plotting.

Finding a true follow-up to Netflix's Dark is hard because most sci-fi shows rely on disposable weekly cliffhangers rather than pre-planned geometry. If you want a complete multi-era time puzzle, start with Bodies (8 episodes) or 12 Monkeys (4 complete seasons). If you want the closest 90-minute movie equivalent, watch Predestination or Coherence tonight.

Part 1: Multi-Timeline & Puzzle-Box Series Like Dark

1. Bodies (2023) — 8-Episode Limited Series

Key Details: 8 Episodes • Complete Ending • Genre: Crime / Time-Loop Mystery • Open Title Details →

Why It Fits Dark Fans: Four detectives across four different eras in London (1890, 1941, 2023, and 2053) discover the exact same murder victim in Longharvest Lane. Like Dark, every era's seemingly isolated clue locks into a single overarching causal loop by Episode 6—and the whole mystery resolves cleanly in one weekend.

2. 12 Monkeys (2015–2018) — 4 Seasons (47 Episodes)

Key Details: 4 Seasons • Complete Planned Finale • Genre: Time-Travel Sci-Fi • Open Title Details →

Why It Fits Dark Fans: Easily the closest long-form cousin to Dark on television. What begins as a straightforward mission to stop a viral outbreak evolves into an intricate tapestry of temporal paradoxes, hidden family lineages, and red-forest mythology—culminating in one of TV's most satisfying planned endings.

3. Severance (2022–Present) — Workplace Puzzle-Box Mystery

Key Details: TV Series • Creator: Dan Erickson • Read Full WatchPulse Review →

Why It Fits Dark Fans: There is no time travel here, yet Severance captures the exact paranoid, small-town dread and meticulous hallway symmetry of Winden. Every line of dialogue and visual prop hides a clue about Lumon Industries' true purpose.

4. Devs (2020) — 8-Episode Deterministic Thriller

Key Details: 8 Episodes • Complete Miniseries • Director: Alex Garland • Open Title Details →

Why It Fits Dark Fans: Created by Ex Machina filmmaker Alex Garland, this hypnotic 8-hour thriller tackles the exact philosophical question at the core of Dark: if cause and effect are mathematically deterministic, does free will exist? Visually austere with an unforgettable score.

Part 2: Feature Movies That Capture Dark in Under 2 Hours

5. Predestination (2014) — 97 Minutes

Key Details: 97 min • Starring: Ethan Hawke, Sarah Snook • Open Title Details & Trailer →

Why It Fits Dark Fans: Built directly on the grandfather/bootstrap paradox that drives Season 2 and 3 of Dark. An undercover temporal agent attempts one final assignment to stop the elusive "Fizzle Bomber"—leading into the tightest puzzle-box twist in modern sci-fi cinema.

6. Coherence (2013) — 89 Minutes

Key Details: 89 min • Micro-Budget Sci-Fi Mystery • Open Title Details & Trailer →

Why It Fits Dark Fans: Instead of jumping across decades, Coherence locks eight dinner-party guests in a single suburban neighborhood as parallel realities overlap beneath a passing comet. For more lean suspense under 100 minutes, see our 10 Tight Thrillers Under 100 Minutes Guide.

7. Primer (2004) — 77 Minutes

Key Details: 77 min • Director: Shane Carruth • Hard Time-Travel • Open Title Details →

Why It Fits Dark Fans: Written and directed by a former engineer, two garage inventors accidentally build a time loop box—and refuse to explain the mechanics to the audience. Like Winden's family trees, you will probably want a notebook on your second rewatch.

8. Arrival (2016) — 116 Minutes

Key Details: 116 min • Director: Denis Villeneuve • Compare in Smart Sci-Fi Guide →

Why It Fits Dark Fans: What makes Dark resonate long after its timeline diagrams fade is tragedy—grief, love, and knowing how a story ends before it begins. Arrival achieves that exact emotional punch in two unforgettable hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

What TV show is closest to Dark on Netflix?

12 Monkeys (the complete 4-season series) and Bodies (2023 limited series) are the closest structural matches to Dark—both weave multi-era causal loops and family secrets into a complete ending. For atmospheric mystery from Dark's creators, watch 1899, or try Severance for a slow-burn puzzle-box thriller.

What movie is most like Dark?

Predestination (2014) and Coherence (2013) capture Dark's tight bootstrap-paradox and reality-splitting mystery in under 100 minutes, while Arrival (2016) matches its emotional weight around time and grief.

Which shows like Dark have a real ending without cancellation cliffhangers?

Bodies (8 episodes), 12 Monkeys (4 seasons), Steins;Gate (24 episodes), and Devs (8 episodes) all tell self-contained stories with intentional finales.
