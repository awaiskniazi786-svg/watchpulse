What to Watch If You Loved Knives Out: 8 Clever Whodunits & Ensemble Mysteries Compared

By Awais K • Availability Last Checked: November 20, 2026

What Makes a Great Whodunit Fun Instead of Stuffy?

Viewer Question Answered: "I loved the witty dialogue, wealthy suspects, and fair-play clues of Knives Out and Glass Onion—what other murder mysteries hit that same sweet spot of clever and entertaining?"

Rian Johnson's Benoit Blanc films reminded audiences that a murder mystery doesn't need grim serial-killer misery to stay gripping. Great whodunits thrive on three ingredients: a sharp eccentric detective, an ensemble of hilariously guilty-looking suspects, and clues hidden in plain sight. Here are eight standout matches.

1. Clue (1985) — 94 Minutes

Key Details: 94 min • Rated PG • Director: Jonathan Lynn • Starring: Tim Curry, Madeline Kahn • Check Live Regional Streaming →

Why It Connects: Pure lightning-fast screwball comedy set in a stormy 1954 New England mansion where six blackmailed strangers race from conservatory to library as the body count climbs. Tim Curry's breathless third-act sprint through every room explaining the crime is comedy history.

2. Gosford Park (2001) — 137 Minutes

Key Details: 137 min • Rated R • Director: Robert Altman • Written by Julian Fellowes • Check Live Regional Streaming →

Why It Connects: Before Downton Abbey, Julian Fellowes won an Oscar for this razor-sharp 1932 English country-house murder mystery viewed simultaneously through aristocrats upstairs and the servants listening behind every door downstairs—directly echoing the class satire of Knives Out.

3. Game Night (2018) — 100 Minutes

Key Details: 100 min • Rated R • Starring: Jason Bateman, Rachel McAdams, Jesse Plemons • Check Live Regional Streaming →

Why It Connects: A competitive suburban couples' game night spirals into a real kidnapping mystery—except the friends keep assuming every gunfight and crime scene is part of an elaborate paid role-playing game. Directed with slick Fincher-style camera tricks and genuinely hilarious performances.

4. Kiss Kiss Bang Bang (2005) — 103 Minutes

Key Details: 103 min • Rated R • Director: Shane Black • Starring: Robert Downey Jr., Val Kilmer • Check Live Regional Streaming →

Why It Connects: A petty thief accidentally auditions into a Hollywood detective role and gets paired with an abrasive private eye ("Gay Perry", played brilliantly by Val Kilmer) while stumbling into a real Los Angeles conspiracy. Fast, meta, and endlessly quotable.

5. See How They Run (2022) — 98 Minutes

Key Details: 98 min • Rated PG-13 • Starring: Sam Rockwell, Saoirse Ronan • Check Live Regional Streaming →

Why It Connects: Set backstage at the 1953 London West End production of Agatha Christie's The Mousetrap, a jaded inspector and an overeager rookie constable investigate the murder of a brash Hollywood director. Light, charming, and briskly paced under 100 minutes.

6. A Haunting in Venice (2023) — 103 Minutes

Key Details: 103 min • Rated PG-13 • Director/Star: Kenneth Branagh • Based on Agatha Christie • Check Live Regional Streaming →

Why It Connects: The strongest of Kenneth Branagh's Hercule Poirot trio. Drops the CGI grandstanding of Death on the Nile in favor of a tight, gothic Halloween séance inside a decaying Venetian palazzo where a retired Poirot refuses to believe the killer is a ghost.

7. The Nice Guys (2016) — 116 Minutes

Key Details: 116 min • Rated R • Director: Shane Black • Starring: Russell Crowe, Ryan Gosling • Check Live Regional Streaming →

Why It Connects: 1977 Los Angeles noir mystery powered by elite buddy-comedy chemistry between Russell Crowe's hired enforcer and Ryan Gosling's hilariously incompetent private investigator.

8. Brick (2005) — 110 Minutes

Key Details: 110 min • Rated R • Written & Directed by Rian Johnson • Starring: Joseph Gordon-Levitt • Check Live Regional Streaming →

Why It Connects: Rian Johnson's debut feature proves his love for classic detective fiction predates Knives Out by 14 years—transplanting hardboiled 1940s Dashiell Hammett slang and mystery plotting straight into a modern California high school.

Quick Comparison: Pick the Exact Whodunit Vibe You Want

Not every mystery fan wants the exact same balance of comedy and tension. Use this quick matrix to match your night's mood before checking where each film streams in your region:

Editorial Pick for First-Time Viewers: If you loved Knives Out mainly for the cozy mansion atmosphere and biting commentary on rich relatives, start with Gosford Park or See How They Run. If you laughed hardest at Benoit Blanc trading absurd one-liners with panicked suspects, queue up Clue or Game Night first.
