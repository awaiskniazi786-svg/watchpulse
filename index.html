Frequently Asked Questions About WatchPulse

How does WatchPulse source movie and TV details?

WatchPulse combines TV series data from the TVmaze API (CC BY-SA), open film metadata from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA) and Wikidata (CC0 Public Domain), our own hand-curated movie notes, and 20 original editorial guides. WatchPulse does not track live per-country streaming availability, so always confirm current playback inside your local streaming app.

Can I watch official trailers on WatchPulse?

Yes! Clicking Watch Trailer plays verified official studio and network trailers inside our privacy-enhanced YouTube modal (youtube-nocookie.com), or links directly to YouTube when an official channel embed cannot be confirmed.

How can I save movies and shows to watch later?

Click the 🔖 Bookmark icon on any card or inside the details modal to add it to your personal Watchlist. Your watchlist and watched status are saved locally in your browser via localStorage with no account required.
