Frequently Asked Questions About WatchPulse

How does WatchPulse organize movies and TV series?

WatchPulse combines live TV series data from the TVmaze API (CC BY-SA) with our own hand-curated editorial movie library and 20 long-form watching guides. Because regional streaming catalogs change frequently, always verify final availability inside your local streaming app.

Can I watch official trailers on WatchPulse?

Yes! Featured titles include an in-page privacy-enhanced YouTube trailer modal (youtube-nocookie.com), and every other title includes a direct link to search the official studio trailer on YouTube.

How can I save movies and shows to watch later?

Click the 🔖 Bookmark icon on any card or inside the details modal to add it to your personal Watchlist. Your watchlist and watched status are saved locally in your browser via localStorage with no account required.
