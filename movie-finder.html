Frequently Asked Questions

How does the WatchPulse Movie Finder Quiz choose picks?

For movies, the picker scores WatchPulse's hand-curated 70-film editorial catalog by who is watching, emotional vibe, runtime window, and original language—surfacing either one laser-focused match or your Top 3 with clear editorial reasons. For TV series, it scores live series metadata from TVmaze (CC BY-SA).

Do I need to sign up or install an app to save my picks?

No account is required. You can copy a direct shareable link to your exact filter combination or open any recommended title in the WatchPulse Discovery Catalog to watch its official YouTube trailer and save it to your browser's local watchlist.

Does WatchPulse stream full movies or track live country availability?

No. WatchPulse is strictly an editorial discovery guide. We embed verified official trailers via YouTube and share reference metadata, so always verify current playback inside your local streaming app.
