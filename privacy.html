Legal & Transparency

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 21, 2026

1. Introduction & Independent Service Status

Welcome to WatchPulse ("we", "our", or "us"), available at watchpulse.online. WatchPulse is an independent movie and TV discovery guide and editorial publication—we are not a streaming provider and are not affiliated with Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+, Apple TV+, HBO Max, or other listed platforms. This Privacy Policy transparently explains what data our site collects, how cookies and analytics work, and your privacy choices.

2. Information We Actually Collect

WatchPulse does not require user accounts, usernames, or passwords. We collect only the minimum data needed to operate and improve the site:

  • Local Browser Storage (localStorage): Your saved Watchlist (including "watched" status) and cookie consent choice are stored locally inside your own browser. This data stays on your device and is not uploaded to a WatchPulse user database.
  • Google Analytics & Tag Manager (GTM-PTPLM5VB) & Consent Mode v2: We use Google Analytics to measure aggregate traffic trends and user interactions (such as page views, search queries, title modal opens, trailer clicks, and watchlist saves). By default, Google Consent Mode v2 initializes ad_storage, ad_user_data, ad_personalization, and analytics_storage to denied until you grant consent.
  • Voluntary Contact & Data-Report Submissions: When you submit a message or report a catalog correction via our Contact Page (powered by Netlify Forms), we receive the name, email address, subject, and message you explicitly enter so we can respond.
  • Standard Hosting Logs: Our hosting infrastructure (Netlify) automatically records basic request metadata (IP address, browser user-agent, timestamp, and requested path) for security, DDoS mitigation, and reliability.

3. Google AdSense, Certified CMP & Cookie Disclosures

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements across WatchPulse to keep the site free for all users. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site. For visitors in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, consent is managed via Google's certified AdSense Privacy & Messaging Consent Management Platform (CMP):

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to WatchPulse and/or other websites on the internet. Learn more about How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting AboutAds.info.

4. Third-Party Services & Metadata

WatchPulse interfaces with the following public third-party APIs to deliver discovery features:

  • TVmaze & Wikipedia Open APIs: We use the public TVmaze API (CC BY-SA) for live TV series listings and Wikipedia open search for film reference summaries. No personal user data is sent to these APIs.
  • YouTube Embeds: Official trailers are embedded using YouTube's privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com) or opened directly on YouTube. By watching trailers, you interact with YouTube under the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

5. CCPA & GDPR User Rights

Under regulations such as the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), users have rights regarding their data:

  • The right to know what data is collected.
  • The right to request deletion of personal information.
  • The right to opt-out of personal data sharing and targeted advertising.

Because WatchPulse does not maintain user databases or sell personal data, your privacy is safeguarded by design.
