Legal & Transparency

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 19, 2026

1. Introduction & Independent Service Status

Welcome to WatchPulse ("we", "our", or "us"), available at watchpulse.online. WatchPulse is an independent movie and TV streaming discovery guide and editorial resource—we are not a streaming provider and are not affiliated with Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+, Apple TV+, Max, or other listed platforms. This Privacy Policy transparently explains what data our site collects, how cookies and analytics work, and your privacy choices.

2. Information We Actually Collect

WatchPulse does not require user accounts, usernames, or passwords. We collect only the minimum data needed to operate and improve the site:

  • Local Browser Storage (localStorage): Your saved Watchlist (including "watched" status), preferred streaming country/region code, and cookie consent choice are stored locally inside your own browser. This data stays on your device and is not uploaded to a WatchPulse user database.
  • Google Analytics & Tag Manager (GTM-PTPLM5VB): We use Google Analytics to measure aggregate traffic trends and user interactions (such as page views, search queries, title modal opens, trailer plays, region/language filter switches, and watchlist saves). This helps us identify broken flows and improve content quality. When you click "Necessary Only" on our consent banner, we store your opt-out preference in localStorage.
  • Voluntary Contact & Data-Report Submissions: When you submit a message or report an availability issue via our Contact Page (powered by Netlify Forms), we receive the name, email address, subject, and message you explicitly enter so we can respond.
  • Standard Hosting Logs: Our hosting infrastructure (Netlify) automatically records basic request metadata (IP address, browser user-agent, timestamp, and requested path) for security, DDoS mitigation, and reliability.

3. Google AdSense & Cookie Disclosures

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements across WatchPulse to keep the service free for all users. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site:

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to WatchPulse and/or other websites on the internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting AboutAds.info.

4. Third-Party Services & Metadata

WatchPulse interfaces with the following authorized third-party APIs to deliver movie discovery features:

  • The Movie Database (TMDB): We use TMDB's public API to fetch movie summaries, release dates, genre tags, and cast information. TMDB does not collect your personal data through our service.
  • YouTube API: Official 4K and HD trailers are embedded using YouTube's standard privacy-enhanced player. By watching embedded trailers, you interact with YouTube under the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

5. CCPA & GDPR User Rights

Under regulations such as the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), users have rights regarding their data:

  • The right to know what data is collected.
  • The right to request deletion of personal information.
  • The right to opt-out of personal data sharing and targeted advertising.

Because WatchPulse does not maintain user databases or sell personal data, your privacy is safeguarded by design.
