Legal & Transparency

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 21, 2026

1. Introduction & Independent Service Status

Welcome to WatchPulse ("we", "our", or "us"), available at watchpulse.online. WatchPulse is an independent movie and TV discovery guide and editorial publication—we are not a streaming provider and are not affiliated with Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+, Apple TV+, HBO Max, or other platforms mentioned in our articles. This Privacy Policy explains what data our site processes, how cookies and consent work, and how you can control your preferences.

2. Information We Collect & Process

WatchPulse does not require user accounts, usernames, or passwords. We process only the minimum data needed to run the site:

  • Local Browser Storage (localStorage): Your saved Watchlist (including "watched" status) and cookie preference are stored locally inside your own browser. This data remains on your device and is never uploaded to a WatchPulse user database.
  • Google Analytics & Tag Manager (GTM-PTPLM5VB) with Consent Mode v2: We use Google Tag Manager and Analytics to measure aggregate site traffic and feature interactions. Before any Google tags fire, Google Consent Mode v2 initializes ad_storage, ad_user_data, ad_personalization, and analytics_storage to denied by default until you grant consent.
  • Voluntary Contact Submissions: When you submit a message via our Contact Page, your form submission is routed securely through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) to our support inbox so we can respond to the name, email address, subject, and message you explicitly provide.
  • Standard Hosting & Edge Logs: Our hosting and CDN infrastructure (Cloudflare Pages / Cloudflare) processes standard HTTP request metadata (IP address, user-agent, timestamp, and requested URL) to deliver pages quickly, mitigate DDoS attacks, and ensure reliability.

3. Google AdSense, Certified CMP & Managing Your Consent

WatchPulse integrates Google AdSense (which displays advertisements once ad serving is active on the site) to support our platform and keep our guides free for everyone. When advertisements are served, Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to WatchPulse and/or other sites on the internet. Learn more about How Google uses information from sites that use our services.
  • Consent Management Platform (CMP) & Consent Mode v2: For visitors in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, consent is requested and managed via Google's certified AdSense Privacy & Messaging CMP before advertising or analytics cookies are enabled. For visitors outside those regions, WatchPulse displays a single lightweight Consent Mode v2 preference bar. Selecting "Necessary Only" keeps ad and analytics storage set to denied.
  • Changing Your Consent Choice Anytime: You can update or withdraw your consent at any time by clicking the Privacy Settings link in the footer of any WatchPulse page, which reopens the consent dialog.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting AboutAds.info.

4. Third-Party Services & Open Metadata APIs

WatchPulse fetches public entertainment metadata through our Cloudflare Pages Edge proxy from the following open services (no personal user account data is sent to them):

  • TVmaze API (CC BY-SA): Provides TV series schedules, primary network tags, cast lists, and show posters.
  • Wikipedia API (CC BY-SA) & Wikidata API (CC0 Public Domain): Provide open film summaries, directors, cast, genres, runtimes, release dates, IMDb reference IDs, and film artwork.
  • YouTube Embeds: Official trailers are embedded using YouTube's privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com) or opened directly on YouTube. By playing embedded trailers, you interact with YouTube under the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

5. GDPR & CCPA User Rights

Under regulations such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have the right to know what data is collected, request deletion of personal information you submitted via our contact form, and opt out of personalized advertising. Because WatchPulse does not maintain user accounts or sell personal data, you can clear local watchlist data directly in your browser settings or adjust cookie consent via Privacy Settings.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your data rights, please contact the WatchPulse Editorial & Privacy Team via our Contact Page or email contact@watchpulse.online.
